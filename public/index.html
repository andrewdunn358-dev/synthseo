What it does

The whole marketing job, without the agency retainer.

Most small businesses know they should be "doing more marketing" and don't have the time to run SEO, content, social and ads as separate jobs. SynthSEO runs all four as one connected system.

Automated SEO audits

Site health, page speed, broken links, missing metadata and local listing accuracy scanned on a schedule — not a one-off PDF that goes stale in a week.

Ongoing content

Regular, locally-relevant articles and pages generated and reviewed each month, so the site keeps giving search engines fresh reasons to rank it.

Social media management

A regular posting schedule across the platforms that matter for the business, drafted, scheduled and reviewed without it eating someone's Friday afternoon.

Ads & PPC management

Campaigns set up, budgets watched and underperforming ads flagged, with spend tracked against actual leads rather than clicks alone.

Ranking & performance tracking

Keyword position, local pack visibility, social reach and ad ROAS tracked over time against real competitors, not vanity numbers.

Client-ready reporting

One plain-English monthly report covering SEO, content, social and ads that a client actually reads — built for MSPs who want to show marketing value without hiring a specialist.
